My first summer on frac sites, in Colorado

My first summer on active frac sites in Brighton, Colorado. I developed an RFID concept for sand logistics and drafted a safety document to keep people out of the red zone.

Brighton, Colorado · Summer 2025

My first summer at Halliburton was my first time doing engineering outside a classroom. As a field engineer intern in Production Enhancement, I worked on active frac sites in Brighton, Colorado.

I worked with the field crews and learned the equipment and procedures. Running a job safely takes a lot of coordination. I picked two problems and worked on each.

  1. 01

    Sand logistics

    An RFID-based automation concept for sand logistics, to cut manual errors and track deliveries more accurately.

  2. 02

    Red-zone safety

    A safety management document to reduce how often people enter the red zone, the high-pressure area around equipment at a frac site.
