Services

How I work

I take 2 projects at a time. Care plans don’t count toward that. I confirm each price in writing once I have reviewed your project.

Fixed prices

Quoted in writing before work begins. If a job takes longer than I estimated, the price stays the same.

50% to start

Half the price starts the work and the rest is due at launch. Care plans are billed monthly.

If the project stops

The deposit covers the work done to that point, and you keep everything that was finished.

Two rounds of changes

Included with every package. Requests after that are quoted before I start them.

Rough timelines

Each package shows a rough timeline. Exact dates go in your written quote.

Not included unless agreed

Writing your copy, photography, domain fees and third-party subscriptions.

Your approval first

Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

You own the work

Code and designs built for you belong to you.

Clear boundaries

Legal and compliance decisions stay with your advisors. I build what they approve.
