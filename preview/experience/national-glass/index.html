National Glass Services

At 20, leading crews across 12 school districts

Project Manager · Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas · Fall 2023 to Spring 2024

15,000+square feet installed by hand

I was 20 years old, leading installation crews of people in their 40s and 50s, many of them with about as many years in the trade as I had been alive.

We put safety film on school windows to meet new Texas security requirements. The crews had up to 25 people each, and we worked in 12 school districts across the Dallas-Fort Worth area.

I directed the crews and worked with school administrators on schedules and access. I ran the jobsite workflow, the measurements, quality control and customer communication, and I installed more than 15,000 square feet of film myself.
