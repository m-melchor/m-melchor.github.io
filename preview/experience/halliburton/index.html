2ndsummer with Halliburton

My second summer in Production Enhancement was in Odessa and Midland, in the Permian Basin. I was back on active frac sites working with field crews. This time I knew the equipment and procedures well enough to start fixing problems the crews ran into daily.

The experienced people in the field taught me a lot. I could understand a system on paper and still not know how it worked in practice until I watched it run.

Parts tracking

I designed and built a parts-tracking and inventory-planning system for frac valve actuators. Teams could see what was deployed in the field, what was in use, and the purchasing and pricing history, all in one place.
