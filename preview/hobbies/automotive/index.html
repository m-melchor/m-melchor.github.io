The M2

I drive my cars, work on them and research parts for them, mostly to understand why one setup behaves differently from another.

My current project is a 2025 BMW M2. So far it has:

  • A satin black wrap in Avery Dennison film
  • New wheels
  • A carbon fiber hood
  • Carbon mirror caps
  • A rear spoiler
  • Yellow daytime running lights
  • Intake upgrades
  • A lot of smaller changes

I like the planning as much as the install: finding the right part, making sure it fits the car, then putting it on.

The Bronco

The Bronco was my first vehicle, and it's where my passion for cars grew deeper.

I worked on it for countless hours. Along the way I built great relationships with:

  • Avery Dennison
  • Diode Dynamics
  • Dyna Performance
  • Kustom Interior
  • Metro Restyling
  • DV8 Offroad

I took it all over Texas, to Colorado, and across New Mexico and Arizona. It gave me so many memories and late nights with friends.

  1. Texas
  2. Colorado
  3. New Mexico
  4. Arizona

Every part I added made it feel more like mine, and made my love for cars grow.

Car events

I go to car events when I can, including Cars and Coffee and local meets.

At the meets I talk to the owners, builders and shops, and not only look at the cars.

Where it led

Both builds ran on the same loop.

  1. 01Find the right part
  2. 02Make sure it fits
  3. 03Buy it
  4. 04Put it on
  5. 05Plan the next one

Apollo Companion™ is that loop turned into software. Every part in a build moves through four stages, with its brand, price and link kept beside it.

  1. Idea
  2. Researching
  3. Purchased
  4. Installed

The meets shaped the rest. The owners, builders and shops I talk to there are who the community, the marketplace and the shop tools are built for.
