Hobbies

Two volcanoes and a lot of trail.

The two biggest hikes I've done are Pico de Orizaba in Mexico and Volcán de Fuego in Guatemala. I also hiked more than 300 miles on Scout trips.

Hiking

Pico de Orizaba

Puebla and Veracruz, Mexico · December 2018

In December 2018 I climbed Pico de Orizaba with my sister. It's the highest mountain in Mexico, and it also goes by its Nahuatl name, Citlaltépetl.

We went up the north side, where the last part of the climb is the Jamapa Glacier: a long, steep slope of snow and ice that runs up toward the crater rim.

  • 5,636 m18,491 feet, the highest point in Mexico
  • 3rdhighest peak in North America, and its highest volcano
  • 1846the last time it erupted

Volcán de Fuego

Near Antigua, Guatemala

In Guatemala I hiked Volcán de Fuego, one of the most active volcanoes in the world. It sits on a ridge with its twin, Acatenango, near Antigua, and it sends up small bursts of ash and lava many times a day.

  • 3,763 m12,346 feet at the summit
  • 2002when its current eruption began, and it hasn't stopped
