Seven mountains in three states.

Every place I've skied, in Colorado, New Mexico and California, with one thing worth knowing about each.

Skiing

Where I've skied

Aspen Snowmass

Colorado

Four mountains on one lift ticket: Aspen Mountain, Aspen Highlands, Buttermilk and Snowmass.

Photo: Aspen Skiing Company, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Vail

Colorado

More than 5,300 acres of terrain, including the Back Bowls on the far side of the mountain.

Breckenridge

Colorado

The Imperial Express on Peak 8 tops out at 12,840 feet, the highest chairlift in North America.

Photo: Entplex, CC BY 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons

Taos Ski Valley

New Mexico

The top of the mountain is Kachina Peak, at 12,481 feet.

Photo: Paul Sableman, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Ski Santa Fe

New Mexico

The base sits at 10,350 feet, one of the highest bases in North America.

Ski Apache

Ruidoso, New Mexico

Owned and run by the Mescalero Apache Tribe, on the slopes of Sierra Blanca.

Photo: Jonathan Cutrer, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Mammoth Mountain

California

The summit is at 11,053 feet, and the season often runs into June.

Photo: Murray Foubister, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons
