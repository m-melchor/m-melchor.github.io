My Eagle project: Feather Lake

Feather Lake is a 43.5-acre wildlife sanctuary in El Paso, built around a wetland the city made in 1969 to hold stormwater. Salt cedars had spread across it, and they soak up water the rest of the wetland needs.

In 2020 I planned and led a cleanup to pull out the salt cedars and clear the trash. I worked out the plan with the sanctuary, brought in Scouts and friends, and ran the safety briefings. It was during COVID, so masks and distance were part of the plan. I put 85.5 hours into it, from planning to the last shift.
