My story

The path here

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Baylor University, with minors in Mathematics and Entrepreneurship. I started in electrical and computer engineering and switched to mechanical, but I kept working with technology.

Since then I've worked in oilfields, spent time in research labs, managed crews, helped start a student organization and built my own automotive technology company.

School

Baylor University

Major

Mechanical engineering

Minors

Mathematics, Entrepreneurship

Where it comes from

I grew up watching my mom and grandmother work hard for our family, and a lot of my motivation comes from that. They worked to make my opportunities possible, and I want to make something of them.

What I like about it

I like the technical side of engineering. I also like working with people, making decisions and watching something come together. I don't like waiting around for an idea to become someone else's project.

There's still a lot I'm figuring out.
