Launch night

16 accounts in about two hours, launching with the Baylor Driving Club on September 14, 2026. By the next morning, 40 accounts. Within 24 hours, 22 of those 40 had used Apollo chat.

Growth so far

As of October 24, 2026, with my own and test accounts left out: 98 members, 74 of them with a car, 93 vehicles, 465 parts logged and 15 clubs.

The plan is drivers and clubs first, then shops. Clubs bring members, and members bring shops.

Reaching more drivers

I'm working to make Apollo Companion™ available to more drivers.

Spanish is part of that: the home page, builds, the map, clubs and the pages for shops and brands all read in Spanish.

In Spanish

The company

Apollo Phoenix Ventures LLC, a Texas company. Apollo Companion™ has been on Google Play since September 8, 2026 and on the App Store since September 13, 2026.

Partnerships with car communities are in early talks.
